重要考試規則

IELTS 學術組 Task 1：至少 150 字、建議約 20 分鐘。評分構面：Task Achievement、Coherence and Cohesion、Lexical Resource、Grammatical Range and Accuracy。Task 2 比重約為 Task 1 的兩倍。

依據：IELTS 官方寫作格式。本教材為非官方教學資源；練習題資料均為虛構。

第一步｜20 分鐘 SOP

0–2 分鐘：讀題

辨識類型、標題、單位、年份；抓最高、最低、變化與例外。

2–5 分鐘：Overview

先寫 1–2 句主要趨勢，避免一開始就羅列數字。

5–17 分鐘：成文

Introduction → Overview → Body 1 → Body 2。以比較組織數據。

17–20 分鐘：檢查

數字、百分點、單位、時態、主詞動詞一致與字數。

第二步｜四段式寫作框架

  1. Introduction：以自己的字詞改寫題目。
    The chart illustrates / compares ... over the period from ... to ... .
  2. Overview：描述全局最大特徵，不先堆數字。
    Overall, ... . Another notable feature is that ... .
  3. Body 1：選出重要組別或起點。
    At the beginning of the period, ... , whereas ... .
  4. Body 2：延續比較、轉折或終點。
    In contrast, ... . The corresponding figure for ... was ... .

注意：上列句型是結構支架，不是可以原封不動套用在每一道題的標準答案。

第三步｜六大題型與原創示範

下列為短版解析，適合手機快速複習。完整文章練習須自行補足至至少 150 字。

01 · Line graph

折線圖｜通勤方式

原創練習資料：汽車占比 2015 年為 60%，2025 年下降至 42%；大眾運輸從 25% 升至 36%；自行車從 15% 升至 22%。

Overview 示範

Overall, cars remained the dominant mode of transport, but their share declined steadily. Meanwhile, public transport and cycling both became more common.

Body 示範

In 2015, cars accounted for 60% of commuting journeys, compared with 25% for public transport and 15% for bicycles. By 2025, the figure for cars had dropped to 42%, whereas the proportions for public transport and cycling had risen to 36% and 22%, respectively.

寫作練習：依資料補上 Introduction，再延伸第二段 Body，整合成至少 150 字的完整文章。PDF 版附有完整原創範文。

02 · Bar chart

長條圖｜圖書館使用

原創練習資料：每月造訪人次（千人）2020→2025：18–29 歲 15→18；30–49 歲 25→27；50 歲以上 22→35。

Overview 示範

Overall, visits increased across all age groups, with the most substantial growth among people aged 50 and over.

Body 示範

In 2020, adults aged 30–49 made the most visits, at 25,000 per month. By 2025, however, the oldest age group had overtaken them, with 35,000 visits compared with 27,000.

寫作練習：依資料補上 Introduction，再延伸第二段 Body，整合成至少 150 字的完整文章。PDF 版附有完整原創範文。

03 · Pie chart

圓餅圖｜家庭能源

原創練習資料：2010→2025：電力 45%→40%；天然氣 35%→25%；太陽能 5%→25%；其他 15%→10%。

Overview 示範

Overall, electricity remained the largest source of household energy, while solar power experienced by far the most pronounced increase.

Body 示範

Solar energy represented only 5% of energy use in 2010. This proportion rose fivefold to 25% in 2025, matching natural gas, whose share fell from 35%.

04 · Table

表格｜線上課程完課率

原創練習資料：2022→2025：語言 62%→74%；科技 48%→66%；藝術 70%→72%。

Overview 示範

Overall, completion rates increased for all subjects. Technology showed the greatest improvement, although it still had the lowest rate in 2025.

Body 示範

The completion rate for technology courses increased by 18 percentage points, from 48% in 2022 to 66% in 2025. By contrast, the figure for arts changed little, rising from 70% to 72%.

05 · Process

流程圖｜咖啡製作

原創練習資料：採收咖啡果→去果肉→清洗→乾燥→烘焙→研磨→包裝。

Overview 示範

Overall, the process consists of seven main stages, starting with the harvesting of coffee cherries and ending with the packaging of ground coffee.

Body 示範

First, ripe coffee cherries are harvested and their outer pulp is removed. The beans are then washed and dried before being roasted. Finally, they are ground into fine particles and packed in sealed bags.

06 · Maps

地圖｜公園改造

原創練習資料：2010 年北側為停車場、西側草坪、中央步道、東側池塘；2025 年北側改為社區花園、中央增自行車道、東側池塘擴大。

Overview 示範

Overall, the park became greener and more suitable for recreation, particularly through the removal of the car park and the addition of a cycling lane.

Body 示範

By 2025, the northern car park had been replaced by a community garden, while the western lawn remained unchanged. A cycle lane was added beside the central path, and the pond in the east was expanded.
