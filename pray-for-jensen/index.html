Heavenly Father, please grant Mr. Jensen Huang wisdom, health, and peace. May his decisions in work and life reflect humility, kindness, and responsibility. Please care for his family, colleagues, and everyone affected by technological progress. In Jesus Christ's name we pray. Amen.
